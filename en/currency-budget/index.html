Calculation·conversion criteria

Calculates expected foreign currency amount based on directly entered exchange rate·separate fees·preferential assumptions from the total KRW budget for travel.

Applicable fee rate = entered fee rate × (1 − discount rate); converted principal = total budget ÷ (1 + applicable fee rate); expected foreign currency = converted principal ÷ entered exchange rate

Input example

Budget 100,000 won · Exchange rate 1,400 won/USD · Fee 1.75% · Discount 50% → fee approximately 867 won · approximately 70.81 USD

If you enter a budget of 100,000 won, exchange rate of 1,400 won/USD, separate fee rate of 1.75%, and discount of 50%, the applied fee rate is 0.875%. Reporting the won budget as the amount including fee, with deposit 99,133 won, fee 867 won, and expected receipt 70.81 USD, the actual customer exchange rate already includes the fee, so the fee rate is entered as 0%.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the exchange rate discount applied to the calculation?

This is a simple assumption that reduces the additional fee rate to the discount rate. A bank’s exchange discount can apply to the difference between the standard exchange rate and the customer rate, and it differs from actual bank conditions.

Is this the actual amount received from the bank?

No. Real-time exchange rates, bank purchase/sale rates, and cash units are not automatically retrieved. Check the applicable exchange rate and fees of financial institutions just before the transaction.

How to enter if a customer exchange rate includes fees?

If the applied fee is included and a separate fee rate is set to 0%, the fee is not added in this calculation.

Calculation criteria and reference materials

  • Korea Bank — Guidance on foreign exchange benchmark rates

    The bank determines the customer exchange rate based on the reference exchange rate and fees. Bank-specific customer exchange rates may vary. The calculator assumes the user’s input exchange rate and separate fees; it does not retrieve the actual published exchange rate.
