Calculation·conversion criteria

It identifies the minimum sales unit where fixed costs, unit selling price, and variable costs over the same period do not result in a loss.

Break-even quantity = rounding((fixed cost ÷ (selling price per unit − variable cost per unit))).

Input example

Month — Fixed cost of 1,200,000 won · Sale price of 15,000 won · Variable cost of 8,000 won → 172 pieces

If the fixed cost of 1,200,000 won is divided by 7,000 won (subtracting the variable cost of 8,000 won from the sale price of 15,000 won), the result is 171.43 pieces. Rounded up to 172 pieces assuming individual sales. The revenue is 2,580,000 won, and the total input cost is 2,576,000 won. Multi-item, discount, and fees are not calculated.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the break-even sales volume rounded?

Increase the amount to the next whole number that can cover the cost, assuming sale of individual units. Products and services with fractional units must be interpreted according to the actual billing unit.

What happens if the sale price is lower than the variable cost per unit?

Each time you sell, there is no amount left to recover fixed costs, so this input cannot calculate break‑even quantities.

Does VAT or platform fees also include it?

All amounts must be entered using the same tax rate. Taxes and fees are not calculated automatically, so they must be directly included in the unit cost.
