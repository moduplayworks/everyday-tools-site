Instructions and example

Inflow L=area m²×rainfall mm×efficiency. Add daily inflow, spill excess, then supply demand. Initial+inflow=supplied+overflow+final storage.

With 100 m², 80% efficiency, 500 L capacity and initial 100 L, rows (10,200),(0,300),(5,100) give inflow 1,200 L, overflow 400 L, supply 600 L and final 300 L.

Supports 1–366 days, rainfall 0–1,000 mm and demand 0–1,000,000,000 L. Intraday timing, evaporation and leaks are excluded. Efficiency is user supplied; water quality is not assessed.

Frequently asked questions

Does using water before rain change the result?

It can. This model uses inflow, overflow, then demand, so it does not capture storage space freed by earlier use within the day.

Which losses belong in collection efficiency?

Enter a fraction reflecting collection losses such as roof runoff, filtering and first-flush diversion. Tank overflow is calculated separately.
