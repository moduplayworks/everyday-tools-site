Calculation·conversion criteria

Compare the payment amount with the one-time payment discount and the installment fee conditions entered by the user to confirm the expected fee, monthly payment range, and total amount.

Monthly fee rate = annual fee rate ÷ 12; monthly fee = remaining principal × monthly fee rate; principal is evenly distributed over installment months, and the remainder is distributed to the previous cycle; fees are rounded to the nearest unit per cycle

Input example

Payment: 1,000,000 won · 6 months · annual fee rate 15% · one-time payment discount rate 0.5% → installment fee 43,750 won · total installment amount 1,043,750 won · one-time payment 995,000 won

If the purchase price is 1,000,000 won, the term is 6 months, the annual fee rate is 15%, and the lump-sum discount is 0.5%, the principal is split into installments of 166,666–166,667 won. Applying the monthly rate (annual rate ÷ 12) to the remaining principal gives a rounded installment fee of 43,750 won and an estimated installment total of 1,043,750 won. After the lump-sum discount, the price is 995,000 won, a difference of 48,750 won. The estimated first-month payment is 179,167 won, and the last-month payment is 168,749 won.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the installment fee calculated?

Multiply the monthly rate divided by 12 by the remaining principal at the start of each cycle. Divide the principal equally and distribute the remainder to the previous cycle. Round the fee amount to the nearest whole unit.

Why is the card description amount different?

Actual fee rate, usage days, payment date, principal distribution, zero-interest offer, and card company billing rules may vary. Actual conditions can be checked in the card company app or billing statement.

Can we also compare interest-free installment or one-time payment options?

Enter 0 for the annual interest rate, and enter the one-time discount rate for the one-time payment discount. Points, cashback, financing offers, and opportunity costs are not calculated automatically.
