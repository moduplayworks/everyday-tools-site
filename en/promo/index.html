Good to know

Make it fit your placement.

  1. Choose an image. Choose a format that matches your placement. Keep the original proportions and leave all text visible.

  3. Check before publishing. Check the image dimensions and file size limits of your publishing service, and make sure the text and URL are readable on mobile.
