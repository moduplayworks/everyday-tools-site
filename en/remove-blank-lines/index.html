Calculation·conversion criteria

Remove empty lines with no content at once.

Exclude empty lines after removing spaces

Input example

Apple + empty line + pear → 2 lines

Entering A, a blank line, B as separate lines leaves only 2 lines: A and B. Lines with only spaces or tabs are also treated as blank lines.

How to use

  1. Use the provided example value or change the text item.
  2. Click Verify result and check the following result.
  3. Result can be copied if needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you also remove leading and trailing spaces from lines with content?

No. Only empty lines are ignored when determining emptiness; the original content of the remaining lines is preserved. Use line padding before/after cleaning to remove padding from content lines.
