Instructions and example

Number input rows in order and lay out cards with a drawing frame and note area.

The defaults generate 2 Kitchen shot cards, Wide and Close, with frames for your own drawings.

At most 20 shots, scene and shot names up to 20 characters each and notes up to 100. Use comma-free text fields; no image generation or shooting-time totals.

Frequently asked questions

Are storyboard drawings generated automatically?

No. Ordered blank frames and notes are produced for your own drawings or camera compositions.

How is shot order changed?

Reorder the input rows and regenerate. Each row is a separate card even when scenes share a name.
