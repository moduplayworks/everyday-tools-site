Calculation method and units

Estimate concrete volume before pouring from the shape, dimensions, quantity and your entered allowance

Worked example

One slab 4 m long, 3 m wide and 0.1 m thick has a volume of 1.2 m³. With the example waste allowance of 10%, 1.2 × (1 + 10 / 100) = 1.32 m³. If you enter a yield of 0.2 m³ per bag, the estimate rounds up to 7 bags. The allowance and yield are user-entered examples, not universal product recommendations.

Rectangular volume is length × width × thickness or depth; a cylinder uses π × (diameter ÷ 2)² × height. Dimensions are converted to metres before quantity and your allowance are applied. Cubic metres, feet and yards show the same volume in different units.

The model does not account for sloped ground, excavation faces, formwork changes, compaction, shrinkage, pumping or transport losses. It is not a mix, construction or structural design. Check the plans, site and supplier requirements before ordering.

Frequently asked questions

Is a 10% allowance recommended?

No. 10% is an editable example value. Excavation, formwork, compaction, losses, and product or supplier requirements can change the needed allowance. Enter a value for your project and confirm site and supplier requirements before ordering.

How is the bag count estimated?

Enter the finished-concrete yield per bag and its unit from the package. The calculator divides the volume including your allowance by that yield and rounds up. It does not assume a product yield or check actual losses or mix suitability.

Can I use this result for structural or concrete mix design?

No. This estimates the geometric volume of the selected rectangular prism or cylinder. It does not assess loads, footing design, reinforcement, soil, building codes, mix ratios, shrinkage, compaction or transport. Check plans and site conditions with an appropriate professional and supplier.

Calculation criteria and reference materials

  • NIST — Guide to the SI, Appendix B.9: Conversion Factors

    This reference gives volume conversion relationships such as cubic yards to cubic metres. This calculator uses the international foot (0.3048 m) for ft³ and yd³. It does not establish supplier ordering rules or site material requirements.

  • NIST — Revised Unit Conversion Factors: International Foot

    This reference confirms the international foot is exactly 0.3048 m. The calculator cubes that definition to convert ft³ and yd³ to m³. Conversion does not set material yield or site order quantities.
