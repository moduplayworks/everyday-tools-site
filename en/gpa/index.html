How to use and processing criteria

Calculates the grade by weighting the credits and grade conversion points for each subject.

Actual example

4.5 full score, 3 grade A+(4.5) and 2 grade B0(3.0) are (3×4.5+2×3.0)÷5 = 3.90. P 1 grade is added to the credits but excluded from the score denominator.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is P·NP·F grade reflected in the average credit?

Excluded from P and NP scores. P is included in the credit score, NP excluded. F is included in the score denominator as 0, but not in the credit score. If the school rules differ, use the official transcript.

Can I get the cumulative score from the existing score and this semester's grades?

Enter the credits and score reflected by the existing score into the additional settings. Add the total existing score and this semester's total score, then divide by the total credits. If the existing score is a rounded value, the cumulative result is a rounded value. Retaking courses is not automatically applied.

Can I enter my cumulative GPA directly into the target grade calculator?

No. This tool calculates a credit-weighted GPA. The target grade tool uses assessment scores and weights within one course to find the required remaining average on a 100-point scale. It does not automatically convert cumulative GPA or school grading rules.
