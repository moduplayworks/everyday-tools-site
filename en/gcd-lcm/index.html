Calculation·conversion criteria

Find the common divisor and multiple for multiple whole numbers.

Lagrange’s algorithm, lcm(a,b) = a ÷ gcd(a,b) × b

Input example

The greatest common divisor of 12, 18, and 24 is 6, and the least common multiple is 72

The greatest common divisor of 12 and 18 is 6. The least common multiple is 12÷6×18=36, so we can check the interval when both cycles overlap.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can 0 or a negative number be entered?

This tool accepts only positive integers. It does not apply separate definitions for greatest common divisor or least common multiple that include 0. Up to 100 numbers can be calculated together.
