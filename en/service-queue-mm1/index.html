Instructions and example

Utilization=arrival/service rate; mean wait=arrival/(service×(service−arrival)).

Arrival 8 and service 12 per hour give mean wait 10 minutes and total time 15 minutes.

Assumes hourly Poisson arrivals, exponential service, independence, unlimited queue and steady state. Arrival must stay below service rate.
