Frequently asked questions

Is complete linkage a centroid distance?

No. It uses the farthest pair across the two clusters, unlike centroid-based k-means.

Can clustering include repeated coordinates?

Yes. Each row remains a separate observation and repeated coordinates have distance 0. Duplicates are not removed.
