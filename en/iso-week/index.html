Calculation·conversion criteria

ISO week number and the month and day of the week for that week.

Starts on Monday, the first week includes January 4th

Input example

2021-01-01 = Week 53 of 2020

January 1, 2021, belongs to 2020-W53 according to ISO standards. The fiscal year is determined by the month’s Thursday, so it may differ from the calendar year at the beginning of January and end of December.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the calendar app or company’s time different?

This tool follows the ISO rule where Monday start and the week containing January 4th are used as the first week. Differentiate this from organizational rules that use Sunday start or the week including January 1st as the first week.
