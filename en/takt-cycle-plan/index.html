Instructions and example

Takt=available minutes/demand. Whole units per station=floor(available minutes/cycle minutes); stations=ceil(demand/whole units per station).

Takt is 1.5 minutes, required load is 120%, and parallel stations total 2.

Identical stations start together and each completes whole units sequentially; work cannot be split. A cycle longer than available time is infeasible. This is not staffing advice. Decimal inputs use exact fractions up to 30 significant digits, 30 decimal places and exponent magnitude 30. Displayed fractional results round to 12 significant digits; integer counts use exact decisions.
