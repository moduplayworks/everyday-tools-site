Batch product photo editor

Remove plain backgrounds from product photos, align margins and dimensions, and export JPG, PNG and ZIP files locally in your browser.

Product photos and output settings

Product photo settings

JPG, PNG or WEBP: up to 12 MB and 12 million pixels per photo, 48 MB total. Run without files to process the public example.

Only similar-colored pixels connected to the photo border are removed. Complex backgrounds, shadows and backgrounds similar to the product cannot be reliably separated.

JPG quality is reduced to try to meet the target. PNG size is not guaranteed. Results show when the target is exceeded.

Processing size and privacy

Files are not sent to a server. Processing reduces the longest edge to at most 1,600 px before fitting the output dimensions. Small subjects may blur when enlarged. Originals stay unchanged and original EXIF is not copied. Reloading clears inputs and results.

Compare originals and results

Compare the first original with all results. If product details disappear, reduce tolerance or undo background removal, then generate again.

How plain-background product photo batching works

If the remaining subject measures 100×200 px and output is 1,000×1,000 px with a 10% minimum margin, the subject becomes 400×800 px. Top and bottom margins are 100 px, left and right margins 300 px. JPG has a white background; PNG preserves removed-background transparency.

Starting at the border, connected pixels within the selected color tolerance become transparent. The remaining opaque bounds are centered with the chosen minimum margin. Enclosed areas of the same color remain, but matching parts of a product connected to the border may be erased.

Output is limited to 4 million pixels per image and 16 million total. Marketplace compliance and background-removal quality are not guaranteed. Check every result before saving.

Frequently asked questions

Can it remove complex backgrounds?

No. It removes only colors similar to the selected color and connected to the image border. Patterns, shadows or matching product colors may remain or erase product details. Review previews, reduce tolerance or turn removal off.

Is the target file size guaranteed?

JPG quality decreases in steps while dimensions stay fixed. An above-target notice appears if it cannot meet the target. PNG does not lower quality, so you may need smaller dimensions or JPG.

Are my photos uploaded or stored on a server?

Photos are processed only in this browser without upload. Original files remain unchanged while new images and a ZIP are created. Private photos and results are not restored after reloading or navigation.
