Calculation method and criteria

It is a tool that compares the total area and price of each order based on the diameter and number of pans of a round pizza.

Actual example

A 40cm diameter pizza of 1 slice costs 30,000 won, which is approximately 1,256.64cm². At 2,387.32 won per 100cm², buying 2 slices of 28cm pizza at 12,000 won each yields approximately 1,231.50cm², with a lower price per area per 100cm² at 1,948.84 won.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are mixed orders compared?

Place the same pizza order in the same A/B/C option. Sum the area, number of pieces, and price for each option, and add shipping cost once more. All combinations are not automatically searched.

Can inches and cm be combined?

Convert 1 inches to 2.54 cm, then calculate the area using π × (diameter/2)². The screen area uses rounded values, and rankings compare by the ratio excluding π.

Recommend the most delicious or large pizza?

Does not calculate thickness, crust, topping, or flavor. It compares prices based on the same circular area, not ensuring actual food consumption.
