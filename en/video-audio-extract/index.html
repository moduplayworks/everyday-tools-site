How to extract audio from videos

4.008 seconds of public video example: clicking extract audio produces an ~4-second audio result. It is a file with only sound, without a screen, and the storage format and size depend on the codec supported by the running browser.

Only the audio of the video is saved as a new file.

The file is played and recorded in this browser. It does not copy the original screen or metadata, but is recreated in the audio container and codec supported by the browser, depending on Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and the device.

No external links or long videos are supported.

Processes only selected MP4, WebM, MOV, or page public examples. Does not download video platforms URLs and processes up to 100MB·60-second videos. Extraction takes as long as the video playback time.

Frequently Asked Questions

MP3 Can it always be saved as a file?

No. MP3 input is readable by the browser, but MP3 encoding output is not guaranteed by the default browser API. Only formats that can be recorded by actual devices are listed, and use a separate encoder app for MP3 output.

Will I download audio from YouTube or other sites?

No. Only selected MP4, WebM, and MOV files from the same site example are processed. External video platform URLs or protected streams are not provided.

Is the file uploaded to the server?

No. The selected file is played by this browser, and only audio is recorded. Maximum size is 100MB and duration is 60 seconds. Keep the tab open during processing.

Calculation criteria and reference materials

  • MDN — MediaRecorder format support verification and recording

    A criterion to first check the MediaRecorder output format of each device. Support reports do not guarantee actual encoding success or resource availability.

  • MDN — AudioContext media element connection

    Connect the audio and video elements of the selected local audio/video to the browser audio graph and stream target. Video frames are not included in audio output.
