How the Modu Card refund estimate is calculated

Estimate your 2026 K-Pass Modu Card refund by use month, residence region, registered benefit group, fare bands and off-peak fares. Compare the basic, General and Plus modes; eligible members receive the most favorable mode automatically through the official system.

Formula and monthly rules for 2026

  • Basic: monthly eligible fares × your registered refund rate. Add 30 percentage points for eligible off-peak trips.
  • General: subtract the General threshold for your region and group from the total for one trip under KRW 3,000. Negative results are set to zero.
  • Plus: subtract the Plus threshold for your region and group from all eligible fares. Negative results are set to zero.
  • For 2026, reduced temporary thresholds apply for months 4–9; original thresholds apply for months 10–12. The off-peak bonus applies through month 12 use.
  • At least 15 rides per month are generally required. During your first month after joining, you may qualify with fewer than 15 rides.

Worked example

For a metropolitan resident in the general group in October 2026, enter KRW 80,000 for one trip under KRW 3,000, KRW 0 for trips of KRW 3,000 or more, KRW 10,000 in off-peak fares and 44 rides. Basic is 20% of KRW 80,000 (KRW 16,000) plus KRW 3,000 for off-peak, totaling KRW 19,000. General is KRW 80,000 minus the KRW 62,000 threshold, or KRW 18,000. Plus is KRW 0 because fares do not exceed its KRW 100,000 threshold. Basic is highest for this example.

Before you calculate

Check your card statement and official app for eligible trips, boarding times, transfer totals, region and benefit group. Do not include separately ticketed services such as KTX, SRT, intercity or express buses. This calculator cannot access your account or card history, issue a card, enroll you or pay a refund. Results are policy-based estimates and do not guarantee payment.

Information checked on October 16, 2026. Recheck the thresholds and formula when government rules change.

Frequently asked questions

Did the thresholds change from October 2026?

Yes. The temporary 50% reduction in fixed thresholds applies through September 2026 use. Original thresholds by region and group return in October. The additional off-peak rate-based refund separately continues through December use.

Which trips count toward General and Plus?

Count a trip in General when the total fare, including transfers, for one trip is under KRW 3,000. Plus uses all eligible fares, including trips of KRW 3,000 or more. Both fare groups count toward the basic rate-based estimate.

How much is the off-peak bonus?

For eligible trips boarded in months 4–12 of 2026 at 05:30–06:30, 09:00–10:00, 16:00–17:00 or 19:00–20:00, the rate is 30 percentage points above the basic rate. This estimate adds that extra amount to the basic rate-based refund.

Will this estimate match my actual refund?

No. This is an estimate based on your monthly totals and registered group. It does not verify eligible trips, transfer totals, times, enrollment status, rounding or issuer settlement. The operating system automatically calculates the best benefit from your card activity.

Can I receive a refund with fewer than 15 rides in a month?

You generally need at least 15 rides per month; the official guide provides an exception for your first month after joining. You must enroll through the Modu Card app or website and register your card.
