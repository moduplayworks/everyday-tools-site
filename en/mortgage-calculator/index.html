Calculation·conversion criteria

Compare monthly principal and interest with the estimated payoff time and total interest when adding monthly principal, using the home price, down payment, fixed annual rate, and amortization period.

Monthly rate r = annual nominal rate ÷ 1,200; monthly principal and interest = loan principal × r ÷ (1 − (1+r)^−months); each period’s interest is the remaining principal × r, rounded to the currency minor unit.

Input example

Home price 400,000 · down payment 80,000 · 6.5% annual rate · 30 years → loan principal 320,000 and baseline monthly principal and interest of about 2,022.62 currency units

With a home price of 400,000, a down payment of 80,000, a fixed annual rate of 6.5%, and a 30-year term, the loan principal is 320,000. The monthly rate is 6.5% ÷ 12, giving a baseline monthly principal-and-interest payment of about 2,022.62 currency units. Adding 250 to principal each month changes the estimated payoff time, total interest, and period-by-period balance.

Frequently asked questions

Does the payment include taxes and insurance?

No. This estimate covers principal and interest only. It excludes property tax, homeowners insurance, mortgage insurance, HOA, fees, and other housing costs. The CFPB explains that taxes and insurance may be added to the monthly principal-and-interest payment, so check a lender estimate for your actual total payment.

How are the rate and period interest calculated?

The entered fixed nominal annual rate is divided by 12 to get a monthly rate. Interest is applied to the opening balance each period and rounded to the currency minor unit (two decimal places). Variable rates, day-count and payment-date rules, fees, and lender-specific rounding are not modeled.

Is the amortization period the same as the loan term?

No. Here, the amortization period is the full time scheduled to repay the loan. A lender’s contract term and the full amortization period can differ, depending on the lender and country, so check your loan documents.

What changes when I enter an extra monthly payment?

The entered amount is added to principal each month on top of the scheduled payment. The result compares total interest and payoff months with the baseline. The calculator does not check contractual prepayment limits or penalties, so review your loan terms before making extra payments. It does not assess eligibility or affordability, or recommend a loan product.
