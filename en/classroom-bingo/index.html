Instructions and example

Seed-shuffle the list and take enough items for a card; retry duplicate arrangements within a bounded attempt count.

From 12 items, create 3 cards of 3×3 cells; each card contains 9 distinct items.

Up to 100 items, sides of 2–5 and 30 cards. No free cell, win checking or money game is provided; at most 100 retries per card.

Frequently asked questions

Can classroom bingo cards share the same words?

Yes. Items do not repeat within a card, but different cards can share items.

Does the bingo generator insert a free cell?

No. Every cell is filled with an entered learning item.
