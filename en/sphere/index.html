Calculation·conversion criteria

Calculate the volume and outer area of a sphere using radius.

Volume=4πr³/3, surface area=4πr²

Input example

Radius 1: outer area ≈ 12.5663706144

The volume of a sphere with radius 3 is 36π, and the outer area is also 36π numerically. The units of the two results are cubic units and square units, respectively.

Frequently Asked Questions

If only knowing the diameter of a sphere, what should I enter?

Enter the radius divided by 2. A sphere with a diameter of 10cm has a radius of 5cm. To find the volume of a hollow cavity, you need to subtract the volume of the inner sphere separately.
