Cover or mosaic the selected area of the photo with black. Save a copy of the PNG with the covered area.

Cover sensitive content with a wide black background. Mosaic does not prevent content estimation.

Blue border is the selected area. The save button must be clicked for the result to apply.

Supported range and original storage

Input is up to 24 million pixels per unit and 50MB total. The original file is not changed. Moving images are treated as static images, and HEIC is not supported. The result is up to 24 million pixels with each side 6,000px (combined 16,000px). Hiding results is saved as PNG. Original metadata and editing history are not preserved in the result.

How to use and processing criteria

A process of covering or mosaicking a rectangular area not to be shared with pixels from the image.

Actual example

Select the 20px on the left, 10px at the top, and 100×50px area, then click 'Hide Selected Area'. Black masking turns the selected pixels into transparent black, and no blue selection border appears in the result.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Mosaic and black masking in privacy protection?

Mosaic retains color and structure, allowing content to be inferred. Sensitive content can be fully covered with black opaque masking and checked directly in the output. Faces, text, or reflections outside the selection are not found or masked automatically.

Can modify the masked area or revert to the original?

On the current screen, you can edit again by deleting regions or clearing all shadows. For saved PNG black shaded regions, black pixels replace original pixels. The original file is kept separately, so make sure you do not share it together.
