How to use and criteria

A tool that alternates focus and break time directly, starts and completes·records skipped sessions

Actual example

Focus 25 minutes·short break 5 minutes·long break 15 minutes·if 4 cycles are set, focus is completed and a short break is prepared. After the fourth focus, a long break is prepared. The next phase must be started by pressing the start button

Changing the device time may affect the measurement values. Due to browser shutdown, power saving, and notification restrictions, use the device alarm for important appointments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will it be clearly displayed even in the background?

Remaining time is calculated based on the difference between device time and start time, but due to screen lock, power saving, or tab restrictions, the sound may be delayed or not heard. The shutdown sound is off by default; use the sound check after turning it on.

Does the number of focus times increase continuously while sleeping?

Only records one completed current segment. The next focus or break starts directly; multiple sessions are not automatically completed. The skipped focus is not added to the completion count.

Are records stored?

This tab maintains up to 200 sessions. Prepare a CSV text for sharing using ‘Create Record’ now. It disappears when refreshed or navigated, and there are no ads on the focused screen.
