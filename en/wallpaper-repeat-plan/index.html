Instructions and example

Drops=ceil(wall width/roll width). Round height+trim up to a repeat multiple. Half-drop matching alternates starting phase by repeat/2.

A 300 cm wall, 53 cm wide and 1000 cm long roll, height 250 cm, trim 10 cm and repeat 64 cm need 6 drops of 320 cm and 2 rolls for straight matching.

Up to 20 walls and 1,000 drops. Phase restarts per wall; rolls are consumed sequentially. No openings, corner overlap, defects or optimal remnant reuse. Each roll starts at phase 0.

Frequently asked questions

Can I subtract doors and windows from wallpaper area?

This model cuts full-height drops and does not subtract area. Actual cutting around openings requires a separate plan.

Why can half-drop matching need more wallpaper rolls?

Alternating start phases can require discarded alignment length. The result includes alignment waste and each roll’s remnant.
