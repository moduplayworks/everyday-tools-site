How to use the online metronome

An online metronome that uses the time signature denominator note as its BPM unit and plays steady clicks to help you keep time.

In 4/4 at 120 BPM, the denominator-4 note (a quarter note) plays 120 times per minute, one beat every 0.5 seconds. Accent the first beat of each 4-beat bar; 2 subdivisions per beat give 8th-note subdivisions.

  1. Set the tempo, beats per bar and time signature denominator.
  2. Adjust subdivisions, volume and the first-beat accent if needed.
  3. Press Start and practice along with the beat. You can also match the tempo by tapping.

Frequently asked questions

Which note does BPM use?

BPM uses the selected time signature denominator note. For example, in 6/8 at 120 BPM, the 8th note plays 120 times per minute. Changing the denominator changes the beat unit while keeping the number of beats in the bar.

How is tap tempo calculated?

Tempo is estimated from the median of recent tap intervals. Up to eight intervals between 0.2 and 2 seconds are used; taps are not sent to a server or saved.

Will it keep playing if I lock the screen or switch tabs?

No. Playback stops when you switch tabs or lock the screen. Browser and device audio handling and power settings mean beat accuracy and background playback are not guaranteed.

Are settings or practice records saved?

No. Tempo and meter settings reset when you reload the page. Inputs, taps and settings are not included in URLs or analytics events.
