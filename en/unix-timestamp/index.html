Calculation·conversion criteria

Read seconds or milliseconds timestamps in UTC local time.

Time elapsed since Unix epoch 1970-01-01T00:00:00Z

Input example

0 seconds = 1970-01-01T00:00:00.000Z

The 0-second timestamp is 1970-01-01 00:00:00 UTC. The local time on devices at the same moment varies depending on the operating system time zone.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are the 10-digit and 13-digit numbers different?

At the recent time, seconds are typically 10 digits and milliseconds are 13 digits, but do not rely solely on the number of digits. You must check and select the original unit, and this tool does not make assumptions automatically.
