Merge CSV files or texts with matching headers. Align columns, preserve every row, preview results and download CSV locally in your browser.

    Format and limits

    Each input: 1,000,000 bytes, 10,000 rows including headers and 10,000 characters per cell. Batch: 10MiB, 100,000 cells and 100 columns. Headers must be unique, not whitespace-only, and match exactly including case and spaces. Convert other encodings to UTF-8 first.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I merge different columns?

    Column order may differ, but header sets must match exactly. Case and spaces matter. Blank or duplicate names and ragged rows are rejected. Missing columns are not filled and rows are not joined by a key.

    Can exported values look different in a spreadsheet?

    A tab is prefixed to formula-like values and headers. Export also uses a UTF-8 BOM, quoting and CRLF. Protection may change displayed values and cannot prevent formula execution in every app; inspect import settings and results.

    What are the size and encoding limits?

    Only comma-separated UTF-8 is supported. Each input allows 1,000,000 bytes, 10,000 rows including headers and 10,000 characters per cell. The batch allows 10MiB, 100,000 cells and 100 columns. Export is at most 2,000,000 bytes including BOM and protection.
