How to add caption to a photo

Input example

After loading the shape photo from Public example, click create text image. For placement outside the photo, maintain the photo ratio and add a white area to hold both texts. For placement inside the photo, maintain the photo size and reduce the text as needed.

The ratio is maintained; only reduce when the selected size exceeds the maximum. Adding white areas above and below the photo adds extra space. For in‑photo text, reduce the font size so each line fits within half the photo height. Long lines will stop saving after guidance. Hangul and emoji are trimmed in units. Each line is 300 characters or 20 lines or less.

Characters are drawn using a sans-serif font on the device, so their shape or line spacing may vary by device. Maintain the transparency of the PNG photo and fill the transparent pixels in JPG with white. The original file remains unchanged. HEIC, WEBP, and animated PNG are not supported. Public examples are drawn shapes on this site; copyright-free photo templates are not provided. Verify the rights to the photo and text to use.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the photos trimmed or enlarged?

Maintain the ratio and do not trim. Only reduce when the maximum width exceeds the limit; do not zoom in on short photos. The area outside the photo adds to the output height.

Does Hangul or an emoji break in the middle of a line?

Korean, combined characters, and emojis — line break each character group. Preserve manually entered line breaks. Shape and width may vary depending on the device font.

Will photo and caption appear in the result link?

Photo · caption · file name is not included in URL, analysis, or sharing caption. Save the final image directly and send it where needed. Clear input and results on refresh or page navigation.

What should I select between PNG and JPG?

The transparent area can be kept as PNG. JPG transparent areas are filled with white. Since re-encoding is required, the color and size may differ from the original.
