Calculation·conversion criteria

Check which day of the year the date is.

Day of the year = days since January 1 + 1

Input example

2024-12-31 = the 366rd day of the leap year

The year 2024, 3 month 1 day is the 31+29+1=61th day. The remaining days are 366−61=305 days.

How to use

Frequently asked questions

Why is the remaining days at the end of the year zero?

It counts the days remaining after December 31, excluding that day. January 1 is the first day of the year, and leap years are reflected in the calendar calculation.
