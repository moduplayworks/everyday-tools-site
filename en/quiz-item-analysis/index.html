Instructions and example

p=Σitem/n; corrected discrimination=Pearson(item,totalScore−item)

For responses 111 / 110 / 100 / 000, correctness proportions are 0.75,0.5,0.25. Each correlation uses the score with that item removed.

Supports 3–200 respondents and 2–50 items in a complete 0/1 matrix. No missing or partial scores; constant scores make correlation undefined. Not a quality verdict or ability estimate.

Frequently asked questions

Why remove the item from the total score?

It reduces the direct overlap caused by including the item in its own comparison score. The result is a sample item-rest correlation.

Does negative item correlation prove a faulty question?

No. Review sample size, answer key and content together; this tool does not prescribe automatic deletion.
