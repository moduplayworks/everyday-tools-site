Frequently asked questions

Which dates are detected automatically?

Only valid explicit year-month-day dates such as 2026-11-12, 2026/11/12 or 2026 (year) 11 (month) 12 (day) in Korean notation appear as candidates. Enter dates without a year, weekday-based expressions, times and locations manually.

Does uploading a photo add an event to my account?

No. Review the recognized text and event fields, then select the confirmation checkbox to save an ICS file. Import that file yourself. This tool never accesses your calendar account.

How are time zones and daylight saving handled?

The entered IANA time zone is used to convert your local times to UTC. Verify it yourself. Times that are nonexistent or ambiguous during a daylight-saving transition are rejected. Repeating and all-day events are outside this tool’s scope.
