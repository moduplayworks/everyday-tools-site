Calculation·conversion criteria

Compare spread of numeric data and sample/population standard deviation.

Mean deviation = sum of squared deviations ÷ n; sample variance is ÷ (n−1)

Input example

Standard deviation of 2, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 7, 9 = 2

Mean of 2, 4, 6 is 4, and variance is 8. Population variance is 8÷3, population standard deviation is approximately 1.633, and sample standard deviation is √(8÷2)=2.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which standard deviation should be used between the sample standard deviation and the population standard deviation?

If all interested subjects are included, consider the population value; if estimating a larger population with a sample, consider the sample value. The sample is divided by n−1, so at least two data points are required.
