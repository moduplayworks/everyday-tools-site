Frequently Asked Questions

Does changing the screen ratio automatically adjust the position of people or objects?

No auto tracking is performed. Center cutting cuts the video based on the center of the frame, while full view preserves the original and adds padding.

Does the screen get cut when converting vertical to video?

When ‘Show all’ is selected, the entire scene is displayed with the remaining area filled with dark padding. When ‘Fill screen’ is selected, the edges that do not match the ratio are cut off from the center.

Where is my video processed?

The video is encoded as a new frame in the browser without being uploaded to the server. The video quality and processing time may vary depending on device performance and the storage format supported by the browser.

Calculation criteria and reference materials

  • MDN — MediaRecorder

    Re-encoding the capture stream using the browser’s supported container and codec. The supported format and output quality depend on the browser.

  • MDN — Canvas captureStream

    Draw the current video frame onto the local Canvas and capture it as a video stream for browser recording.

  • WebKit — MediaRecorder API

    Describes Canvas·Web Audio stream recording and format detection in WebKit implementation. Current device codec support is detected during execution.
