Calculation method and criteria

A tool that converts the subscription payment cycle into monthly or annual costs and creates a 12‑month renewal schedule starting from the next payment date.

Actual example

Starting from 2026-10-03, 10,000 won per month (next payment on October 10) and 120,000 won per year (next payment on January 31, 2027) total 20,000 won per month and 240,000 won per year. Paying 13 times by 2027-10-02 amounts to 240,000 won.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the monthly adjustment sum different from the period payment sum?

Monthly adjustment is the average of the amount per cycle divided by the number of cycles. The period sum multiplies the payment amount by the actual number of cycles from the reference date to 12 months later. Subscriptions starting after the period are also included in the monthly adjustment.

31How is the monthly and quarterly renewal calculated?

Based on the entered next payment date, the original date is maintained each week. If no such date exists, the end of the month is used, and the next month returns to the original date. It may differ from actual service rules.

Is auto deactivation or alerts also available?

Not linked to account or subscription. Calculate based on manually entered amount and next payment date. Not reflected for cancellation, notifications, exchange rates, free trial, or price changes.
