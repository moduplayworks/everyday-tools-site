Calculation·conversion criteria

Estimate reading time based on manuscript length and assumed reading speed.

Expected minutes = number of frames per blank ÷ frames per minute

Input example

Reading 300 lines at 150 lines per minute is 2 minutes.

Entering 600 lines per minute at 200 lines per minute based on spaces results in 3 minutes. The speed is the user’s assumption; it does not measure manuscript difficulty or comprehension.

How to use

  1. Use the entered example value or change the text to read, or words per minute items.
  2. Click Verify result and check the following result.
  3. Result can be copied if needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Korean character counts be directly applied to English reading speed?

The criterion of this tool is the segment separated by whitespace, not characters. Speed varies depending on language and readability, so use the speed you read directly and interpret the result as a simple estimate.
