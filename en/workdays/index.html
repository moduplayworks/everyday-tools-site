Calculation·conversion criteria

Calculate the number of weekdays excluding weekends and designated holidays during the period.

Includes start and end dates, excluding weekends and manually entered dates.

Input example

2026-10-02 to 12: If no input, it’s a regular weekday with 7 days

From Monday, October 5, 2026, through Sunday, October 11, the standard workweek has 5 weekdays. Excluding October 9 leaves 4 days. Apply actual holidays manually.

How to use

  1. Use the entered example value or change the start date, end date, and excluded date (YYYY-MM-DD, one per line) items.
  2. Click Verify result and check the following result.
  3. Result can be copied if needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Korean legal holidays and substitute holidays automatically exclude each other?

Does not disable automatically. Exclude Saturday and Sunday by default; enter holidays, company holidays, and personal holidays by date. Start and end dates are included if they meet the conditions.

If you enter a weekend exclusion date, will it be missed twice?

No. Weekends and exclusion lists are checked together with each date, so no days are subtracted due to duplicate input or weekend exclusion.
