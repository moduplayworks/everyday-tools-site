How to calculate adult BMI

Enter height in cm and weight in kg, or ft/in and lb, to calculate adult BMI and view WHO general categories for ages 20 and older.

At 170 cm and 70 kg, BMI is 70 ÷ 1.70² ≈ 24.22. The weight range corresponding to BMI 18.5–24.9 is about 53.5–72.0 kg.

How to use

  1. Choose metric or US customary units.
  2. Enter your height and weight, then select Calculate BMI.
  3. View BMI, the general adult categories, and the reference weight range for your height.

BMI ranges and limitations

WHO general adult categories are underweight below BMI 18.5, normal weight at 18.5–24.9, overweight at 25–29.9, and obesity at 30 or higher. The relationship between BMI and body-fat level, and the interpretation of risk, may vary across populations.

BMI is a screening measure that does not directly measure body fat or its distribution and does not diagnose disease. Do not use this result alone to assess health for people with high muscle mass, during pregnancy, or under age 20. Children and teens need a separate assessment using age- and sex-specific growth charts.

The accepted input ranges match the CDC adult BMI calculator. The reference weight range is the mathematical result of applying BMI 18.5–24.9 to your height; it is not a personal target or recommendation.

Frequently asked questions

How is BMI calculated?

Divide weight in kg by height in meters squared. Pounds and inches are converted to metric units before applying the same formula.

Does a BMI result diagnose health status?

No. BMI is a screening measure based on weight relative to height; it does not measure body fat, its distribution, or diagnose an individual health status. Interpretation may vary with muscle mass, pregnancy, age, and population.

Can children and teens use this calculator?

Like the CDC adult calculator, this tool displays categories for people age 20 and older. Children and teens need a separate assessment using age- and sex-specific growth charts.

Calculation criteria and reference materials

  • WHO — Adult BMI classification

    Reference for BMI = kg/m² and the adult categories <18.5, 18.5–24.9, ≥25, and ≥30. WHO notes that BMI may correspond to different body-fat levels and risk interpretations across populations.

  • CDC — Adult BMI Calculator

    Source for the adult BMI calculator scope of age 20 and older, height limits of 3–9 ft and 91.44–274.32 cm, weight limits of 55–1,000 lb, and screening-measure explanation. This tool is not the CDC calculator and does not diagnose.
