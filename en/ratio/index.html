Calculation·conversion criteria
Simplest way to simplify the ratio of two integers.
Divide A:B by the greatest common divisor for simplification.
Input example
1920:1080 = 16:9
1920:1080 divides the two values by the greatest common divisor 120 to get 16:9. The proportion of the first value is 16÷25=64%, and the aspect ratio and proportion in the total are different concepts.

Frequently asked questions
How do I simplify a ratio with decimals?
Multiply both values by the same power of ten to make whole numbers. Enter 1.5:2.5 as 15:25 to get 3:5. This tool simplifies two positive whole numbers.
How are 16:9 and 64% different?
16:9 compares the first value with the second. The first value’s share of the total is 16 ÷ (16 + 9) × 100 = 64%, and the second value’s share is 36%.
Can I enter zero, negative values or more than two values?
This tool accepts two positive whole numbers from 1 to 1,000,000,000. Zero, negative numbers, decimals and ratios with three or more terms are not directly supported.
