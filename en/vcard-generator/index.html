How it works and limits

The Alex Example public example creates contact.vcf and a QR PNG with name, organization, phone, email and website. Enter family and given names separately; clear optional fields you do not need. The tool writes vCard 3.0 N and FN properties and folds long lines without splitting UTF-8 characters. Import the saved file in your contacts app and verify names and numbers.

Frequently asked questions

Are contacts added automatically?

No. Scan the QR or open the vCard, then review and save in your phone or contacts app. Import behavior is not guaranteed for every app.

Can I edit an existing contact QR later?

The contact is embedded directly in a static QR. Generate and share a new file and QR after changes. Hosted dynamic business cards and scan analytics are not provided.

Which contact fields are supported?

Family/given names, organization and one phone, email and HTTP/HTTPS website are supported. Photos, addresses, birthdays and multiple phones are excluded. The complete vCard is limited to 1,200 UTF-8 bytes.
