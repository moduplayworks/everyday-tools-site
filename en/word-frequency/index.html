Calculation·conversion criteria

Check the repetition of lines separated by spaces

Separate by spaces and count the exact number of identical lines

Input example

Apple pear apple → 2 apples

“Apple Apple Pear” is counted as 2 apples and 1 pear. Spaces and line breaks are used as separators.

How to use

  1. Use the provided example value or change the text item.

  3. Result can be copied if needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are similar articles or punctuation marks also combined?

No. It counts “apple,” “apple with a particle,” and “apple!” as separate word units. It does not analyze morphology or meaning, so results vary depending on whether punctuation is removed.
