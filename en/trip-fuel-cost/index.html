Calculation method and criteria

A tool that estimates fuel and travel costs based on distance traveled and fuel consumption.

Actual example

For a trip of 300 km each way, with fuel economy of 12 km/L and fuel at 1,700 won/L, the round trip uses 50 L (600 ÷ 12), costing 85,000 won. Add 20,000 won in tolls and 5,000 won for parking for a total of 110,000 won. Split among 3 people, that is about 36,667 won per person.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are roundtrip and toll fees applied?

If you choose round trips, the input distance will be 2 times larger. Please enter the total travel cost including parking and tolls. This additional fee does not increase by 2 times automatically.

How is fuel consumption compared with the vehicle?

km/L = distance ÷ fuel consumption; L/100km = distance × fuel consumption ÷ 100. Calculate required liters. Compare different fuel and fuel prices under the same vehicle 5 conditions. Common distance and parking fees apply identically.

Are fuel cost and per-person amount accurate real-time values?

Fuel cost is entered directly; no real-time lookup. It does not predict actual fuel consumption based on operating conditions. Intermediate calculations are not rounded; the final amount is rounded to the nearest yuan. The per-person amount is for reference only, so the rounded total may differ from the actual amount
