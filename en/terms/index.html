Features provided

We provide free calculators, unit converters, text utilities, image, PDF and file tools, and study tools. No registration or payment is required. Each tool explains its scope and supported formats.

Range of calculation result

Results follow your inputs and the formulas or processing rules stated in each tool. Units, rounding, date inclusion, file formats and processing limits can affect the result. Check the original data and the explanation together.

Cost, finance, payroll and material estimates depend on the assumptions you enter. Check the applicable criteria and actual records before using results for quotes, contracts, filings or purchases. Signature images and edited PDFs do not certify legal validity or document authenticity.

Store records

Only tools that explicitly offer storage keep records in this browser. Inputs and results in other tools may disappear when you reload. Favorites and recent tools store only tool identifiers, not your input.

Where a tool offers backup or download, use it to keep the copies you need. Keep original files separately before editing and check the output before use. The usage guide explains deletion and backup scope.Usage instructions
