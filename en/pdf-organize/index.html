How to use and processing criteria

Edit order, deletion, and rotation while viewing a real preview of the PDF page.

Actual example

In pages 1, 2, and 3, move page 3 forward two times and delete page 2. The result is pages 3 and 1. One rotation button adds 90 degrees clockwise from the original direction.

Frequently Asked Questions

How to change the order of PDF pages on mobile?

Use forward/backward buttons below the thumbnail. Shows 6 pages at a time, moving to previous or next. The first page cannot move forward, and the last page cannot move backward. The original number is continuously displayed.

Can I revert the deleted page and keep the original file?

Click Page order, delete, and rotation initialization to return to the initial state. Saving creates a new copy, so the original file remains unchanged. At least one page must remain, and electronic signature, bookmark, and attachment preservation are not guaranteed.
