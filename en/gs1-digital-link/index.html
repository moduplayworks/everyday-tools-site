Create GS1 Digital Link QR

Check a GTIN check digit and create a GS1 Digital Link URI and QR code with an optional lot, serial number, and expiry date in your browser.

GS1 Digital Link QR inputs

For a real product, enter its complete GTIN assigned under GS1 rules. The example check digit is validated and the identifier is padded to 14 digits for Digital Link. This tool does not verify assignment or usage rights.

The default is id.gs1.org, used in GS1 standard examples. You can enter an HTTPS address you manage, but ownership, DNS, and resolver setup are not checked.

Lot and serial values use 1–20 GS1 XCHAR letters, digits, or symbols. Characters with special meaning in a URI are percent-encoded.

Under GS1 AI 17, choose a date from 49 years before through 50 years after the current year. The URI stores it as YYMMDD.

The GTIN and optional values stay in this browser. This tool creates a URI and QR; it does not create a product information page or verify the server address, print quality, or GS1 registration.

How to use and processing criteria

Build a GS1 Digital Link URI from a GTIN formatted under GS1 rules and optional lot, serial number, or expiry date, then save it as a QR image.

Actual example

GTIN 9520123456788 passes its check digit and is padded to 09520123456788. Add lot ABC1, serial 12345, and expiry date 2028-04-26 to generate the URI https://id.gs1.org/01/09520123456788/10/ABC1/21/12345?17=280426 and its QR code.

This QR contains a GS1 Digital Link URI. It does not create a Digital Product Passport (DPP) or product page, or check whether your domain serves the right information after scanning. Print it at its intended size and test it with another device and POS system before use.

Frequently asked questions

Why check the GTIN check digit?

The check digit can catch a typing error. The GS1 Digital Link format expresses GTIN-8, GTIN-12, and GTIN-13 as 14 digits by adding leading zeroes. A valid check digit does not prove issuance or ownership of the number.

Does this create a Digital Product Passport (DPP)?

No. It creates a GS1 Digital Link URI and a QR image that contains it. It does not create a product data page or DPP repository, manage access, or check product-specific legal requirements.

Will every store scanner read this QR code?

That is not guaranteed. Readability depends on print size, contrast, package curvature, readers, and point-of-sale setup. Print a sample and test it with the devices and POS systems you plan to use. GS1 Sunrise 2027 is an industry adoption goal for 2D barcodes, not certification of every product or store.

Calculation criteria and reference materials

  • GS1 — Digital Link Standard: URI Syntax 1.7.0

    Reference for padding GTIN-8, GTIN-12, and GTIN-13 to 14 digits, ordering AI 10 and AI 21 in the URI path, and using YYMMDD for AI 17. This tool supports only GTIN and these three optional values; it does not validate every GS1 Application Identifier.

  • GS1 — General Specifications 26.0.0

    Reference for GTIN check digits and the AI 17 date window of 49 years before through 50 years after the current year. The tool accepts a real calendar date and encodes it as YYMMDD under AI 17.

  • GS1 — Barcode Syntax Resource

    Official resources for the GS1 Application Identifier dictionary, syntax tests, and reference engine. Use these resources and the browser demo to check more identifiers or combined formats.

  • GS1 — Why is the goal to transition to 2D barcodes by 2027?

    Explains GS1’s goal to expand 2D barcode acceptance at retail point of sale and the purpose of Sunrise 2027. The target is not a legal obligation for every product or retailer, nor a scanner compatibility certification.
