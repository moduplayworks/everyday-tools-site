Receipt expense CSV

Find date and total candidates in receipt photos. Enter merchants and currencies, check the originals, and save expense CSV. Photos stay on your device.

Receipt photos and settings

Up to 5 photos, 12 MB each / 36 MB total. JPG, PNG or WEBP; at most 6,000 px per side and 12,000,000 pixels.

Photos stay in this browser. The first recognition downloads OCR files from this site. Japanese OCR is unsupported. Reloading clears photos and results.

Edit the public example or choose photos. Review the example to export it as CSV.

Review originals and expenses

Only explicit-year dates and labeled totals become candidates. Ambiguous candidates remain blank. Merchant and currency are never guessed. Check date, merchant, amount and currency against each original. No line-item extraction, currency conversion or tax validation.

Whole units for KRW/JPY; up to two decimals for USD/EUR/GBP. Enter no grouping commas.

New photos selected. Previous results cannot be saved. Run recognition first.

Formula-like merchant names receive a leading apostrophe, so exported text may differ. Protection is not guaranteed in every spreadsheet application.

How it works and limits

Recognized dates and amounts are candidates, not confirmed expenses. Enter merchants manually. Compare the source photo and OCR text, correct the date and final amount, and select the currency. Review each row before saving CSV. For example, KRW 12500 plus KRW 3500 totals KRW 16000; USD amounts are totaled separately.

Frequently asked questions

Are recognized amounts automatically confirmed?

No. OCR may misread totals or dates. Distinguish subtotal, tax and change from the final amount and check the original. Unclear candidates must be left blank or corrected; unreviewed rows cannot be exported.

Does OCR support Japanese receipts?

Photo recognition currently supports Korean and English only. A Japanese interface does not imply Japanese OCR support. Blurred or crumpled receipts may be misread. Handwriting, automatic item classification and tax filing are not supported.

Where are photos and expense details stored?

Selected photos and edited entries stay in current page memory. Names, images and OCR text are not sent to analytics or URLs and are cleared on reload. Downloaded CSV files remain on your device.
