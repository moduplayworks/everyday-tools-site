Frequently asked questions

Can you change the photo order and PDF paper?

You can reorder items in the selection list using the Next/Previous buttons. A4 Letter uses vertical/horizontal/photo scaling. For photo size paper, use the photo ratio based on 96dpi and add margins.

What is the quality and transparent background of the original PDF after organization?

Refine the photo orientation and re-encode at 92% JPG quality. Fill transparent areas with white. No lossless conversion or preservation of original metadata is provided; the original photo file remains unchanged.
