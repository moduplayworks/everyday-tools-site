Instructions and example

Dynamic programming stores the minimum cost for each visited subset and final waypoint, then backtracks the route.

The default optimum is 1→2→3 with distance 2+3=5; returning to the start gives total 14.

Use 2–12 waypoints, integer distances 0–1,000,000 and diagonal 0. Always starts at waypoint 1; no traffic, roads or maps are queried. Decimal results display at most 12 significant digits.

Frequently asked questions

Can outward and return distances differ?

Yes. Asymmetric matrices are supported; each cell is travel from its row to its column.

Does the route revisit intermediate waypoints?

No. Each listed waypoint is visited once; only return mode repeats the starting point at the end.
