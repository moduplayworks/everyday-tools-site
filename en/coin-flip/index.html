Calculation·conversion criteria

Throw coins multiple times to count the number of faces and reverse sides.

Independently extract 0 or 1 from each trial

Input example

When rolling the die 10 times, the sum of heads and tails = 10

When rolling the die 10 times, the sum of heads and tails is 10. Even if 5 rolls don’t occur, it’s not an error, and each result is shown in order.

How to use

  1. Use the provided example value or replace the number of throws field.
  2. Click Verify result and check the following result.
  3. Result can be copied if needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

If the front side appears repeatedly, will the back side appear better next time?

No. Since each trial is extracted independently, the next probability does not change due to previous results. In small trials, the number of sides may vary significantly.
