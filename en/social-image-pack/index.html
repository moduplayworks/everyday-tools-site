Create image sizes for multiple platforms at once

From a 4,000×3,000px photo, select a 3,840×2,160px YouTube video thumbnail, a 1,200×627px LinkedIn link image, and a general 4:5 image to create three files in a ZIP. Adjust the crop position to keep the subject in view. The YouTube channel-art preview includes a safe-area guide for device-dependent display.

Cropping and safe areas

Cover mode crops part of the source to match the chosen ratio. Adjust the horizontal and vertical positions to keep the subject in view. Contain mode keeps the full photo and adds space in the selected background color.

Platforms can display or crop images differently by image type and screen. These sizes create files for selected target dimensions; they do not guarantee account eligibility, policy compliance, or a particular post display. Check the official guidance before uploading.

Example and limits

A 4,000 × 3,000px photo is cropped around the selected focal point to make a 1,200 × 627px link image. The original stays unchanged; the ZIP contains new JPG or PNG copies.

Use one JPG, PNG, or WEBP image up to 20 MB and 24 million pixels. Create up to 8 outputs with a combined limit of 24 million pixels. JPG fills transparent areas with white. New images do not copy the source EXIF photo data.

Frequently asked questions

Can I save image sizes for multiple platforms at once?

Choose up to 8 sizes for one source photo and create individual JPG or PNG files plus a ZIP. Combined output is limited to 24 million pixels; processing time can vary with browser memory and device performance.

Can I reposition the subject in a cropped photo?

Move the crop center with the horizontal and vertical sliders and check the preview. Choose contain to keep the full photo and add background space instead of cropping.

Are the dashed YouTube channel-art guides included in the saved file?

No. The dashed lines and shaded edges are preview guides only; the output contains the photo. YouTube may crop the banner differently by device and screen, so keep important text and logos inside the safe area and check the official upload guidance.

Are these exact Instagram or TikTok specifications?

The 4:5 portrait and 1:1 square options are general ratios, not claimed platform specifications. Supported sizes and cropping can vary by post type, app, and display, so check the service's current guidance before posting.

Calculation criteria and reference materials

  • YouTube Help — custom video and Shorts thumbnails

    Recommended resolutions are 16:9·3840×2160px for videos and 9:16·2160×3840px for Shorts. Check YouTube guidance for account, device, and policy limits.

  • YouTube Help — channel banner image

    The recommended size is 2560×1440px. The text and logo safe area is defined at the minimum size; this preview scales it proportionally to the recommended banner size.

  • LinkedIn Help — Page image specifications

    Recommended sizes are 1512×256px for a Page cover and 1200×627px for a link post image. LinkedIn may crop or scale images depending on device and placement.

  • X Help — Post photos or GIFs

    X says a single photo with a standard ratio from 2:1 through 3:4 displays in full. These are example sizes within that range and do not guarantee X's post specifications or display layout.
