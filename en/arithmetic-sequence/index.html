Calculation·conversion criteria

Find the term of a sequence that increases by a fixed difference and its sum.

aₙ=a₁+(n−1)d, total=n(a₁+aₙ)/2

Input example

First term 1, common difference 2, 5term: sum = 25

If the first term is 3, common difference 2, and number of terms is 5, the sequence is 3, 5, 7, 9, 11. The last term is 11, and the sum is 5×(3+11)÷2=35.

Frequently asked questions

Can empty difference be negative or 0?

Possible. For a deviation of −2, each term decreases by 2; if the deviation is 0, all terms are equal. The number of terms is a positive integer, and the first term is called the first term.
