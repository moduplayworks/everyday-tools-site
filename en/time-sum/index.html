Calculation·conversion criteria

Sum the total duration of multiple tasks or videos.

Convert each line’s time to seconds and sum it

Input example

1:30:00 + 0:45:00 = 2:15:00

45 and 0: 30: 00 put each on a separate line, resulting in 45 minutes + 30 minutes = 1: 15: 00. If only numbers are entered, they are interpreted as minutes, and the minutes and seconds in H:MM:SS must be 0 to 59.

How to use

  1. Use the entered example value or change the time list (one line per H:MM:SS or integer minutes) items.
  2. Click Verify result and check the following result.
  3. Result can be copied if needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can 1:30 be entered as 1 hour and 30 minutes?

This tool does not accept ambiguous two-part times. Enter 1 hour 30 minutes as 1:30:00 or 90. Its format differs from the MM:SS rule in the lecture playback-speed calculator.
