Frequently asked questions

Which files and durations are supported?

Files are limited to 60 seconds and 20MB, and the browser must decode their audio codec. Use the audio extraction tool for unsupported videos. Split longer recordings with audio trimming and process one file at a time.

Can the model invent incorrect words?

Noise, music, quiet speech and silence can cause omissions or hallucinations. Check every caption and start/end time against the source. Speaker identification, live recording and automatic translation are not supported.

Is my recording uploaded?

Input files and captions are not uploaded. On your explicit action, model and runtime files download from this site and processing runs in your browser. Refresh or departure clears inputs and results; model files may remain cached.
