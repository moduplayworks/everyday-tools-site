Frequently asked questions

What does a negative RLC lag angle mean?

When capacitive reactance dominates, current leads source voltage. A positive angle is inductive and means current lags.

Does RLC impedance become 0 at resonance?

This model requires positive resistance. Reactances cancel at resonance, but impedance magnitude remains equal to resistance.
