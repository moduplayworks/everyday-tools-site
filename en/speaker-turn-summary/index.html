Instructions and example

Union intervals per speaker and sweep all event boundaries to measure overlap and silence.

The defaults give Alex 15 seconds, Blair 7 seconds, overlap 2 seconds and silence 5 seconds within 0–25 seconds.

At most 30 speakers and 200 intervals, with times 0–86,400 seconds. No audio analysis, speaker identification, interruption intent or conversation-quality assessment. Decimal results display at most 12 significant digits.

Frequently asked questions

Are a speaker’s overlapping intervals counted twice?

No. They are unioned first. Overlap requires different speakers to be active simultaneously.

Is silence before or after the supplied turns included?

No. Only the earliest start through the latest end is covered; recording length is not inferred.
