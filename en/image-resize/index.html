How to use and processing criteria

This is the process of changing the horizontal·vertical pixels or ratio of a photo file.

Actual example

Resizing a 2,400×1,200 px image to 1,200 px wide produces 1,200×600 px. Fitting it within 800×800 px produces 800×400 px; setting an exact size of 800×800 px changes the aspect ratio.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which mode is used to maintain the horizontal/vertical ratio?

Align horizontally · align within horizontal/vertical ranges · original ratio mode maintains the ratio. Exact size is scaled up or down to pixels, which may distort the photo.

Can you reduce multiple photos to the same original ratio?

Select up to 10 pieces and use horizontal alignment or ratio based on the original. Calculate the ratio separately for each image. The result must be up to 6,000px in width/height and 24 million pixels, and details cannot be restored when zoomed.
