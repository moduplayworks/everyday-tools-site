How to use and criteria

It is a tool that performs multiple counting independently due to differences in device time.

Actual example

If a 5-minute timer starts and stops after 2 minutes, about 3 minutes remain. Continue after excluding the paused time. Adding a second 10-minute timer does not affect the first timer.

Changing the device time may affect the measurement values. Due to browser shutdown, power saving, and notification restrictions, use the device alarm for important appointments.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many at a time can be executed?

Up to 5 pieces, each set between 1 second and 24 hours. Changing the current timer continues other timers, and the remaining time in the total list can be viewed.

Does an alert appear when it ends?

Informs with the page’s completion status and selected end sound. No browser or OS notifications are sent; screen lock or background mode may cause delay.

What happens if you change or delete time?

Before starting, you can change minutes, seconds, and presets. Timers that have started must be reset to change settings, and deletion/reset do not affect other timers.
