Instructions and example

Sowing advances by the interval and includes the end date. Occupancy starts at sowing+nursery; harvest starts at occupancy+growth; occupancy ends at harvest+duration−1. Beds rotate cyclically.

Starting 2026-03-01 every 7 days with nursery 14, growth 20 and harvest 7 days, the initial occupancy is 2026-03-15–2026-04-10. With 2 beds, batches 1 and 3 overlap.

Sowing window spans at most 366 days, with 1–50 beds. The final occupancy day is still occupied. Shows the total conflict count and up to 50 pairs. No climate, frost or crop-growth prediction.

Frequently asked questions

Does a conflicting sowing batch move to another bed automatically?

No. It cycles through the entered beds and reports conflicts unchanged. Compare scenarios by changing bed count, sowing interval or durations.

Does planting on the final harvest day count as overlap?

Yes. Occupancy includes both endpoints. The next batch must enter after the previous final day to avoid overlap. Nursery time is assumed to occur outside the beds.
