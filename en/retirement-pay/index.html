Calculation·conversion criteria

Enter your start date, separation date, and wage details to estimate average wages before separation and Korean statutory severance pay.

Severance pay = wage for 1 day × 30 × days of continuous service ÷ 365. Use the ordinary wage if it exceeds the average wage.

Input example

Start Date: 2023-10-06, Retirement Date: 2026-10-06 (the day after the last work day), 9,000,000 won for 3 months before retirement, 40 hours per week is used as an example.

The general Korean statutory formula is average daily wage × 30 × days of continuous service ÷ 365. For this example, the separation date is the day after the last working day. Average daily wage uses wages paid in the preceding 3 months divided by calendar days in that period; the estimate also includes 3/12 of the prior-year bonus and annual-leave allowance where applicable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you enter the last work day as the retirement date?

No. Enter the next day after the last working day according to the Ministry of Labor and Employment calculator. The calculator treats the previous day as the last three months before retirement.

How do I identify who is eligible for retirement pay?

The general check indicates at least 1 year of continuous employment and an average of at least 15 scheduled work hours per 1-week period over 4 weeks. It does not automatically assess periods below 15 hours per week or changes in employee status.

Was it accurate to include leave, emergency, or maternity leave?

This period can be excluded from average wage calculation or adjusted based on service duration. Since this simple calculator does not automatically adjust this period, please check the official Ministry of Labor calculator and wage data.

Calculation criteria and reference materials

  • Korean Law Information Center — Employee Retirement Benefit Security Act, Article 8.

    Legal standard: Pay retirement benefits equal to 30 days of average wage for continuous employment of 1 year.

  • Korea Ministry of Employment and Labor — statutory severance pay calculator

    Rules for entering the retirement date, guidance on salary and working days in the last 3 months, excluded periods, bonuses, annual leave, and regular salary. This tool simplifies calculations for general salaries but does not handle excluded periods.

  • Korea Ministry of Employment and Labor customer center — severance pay and average-wage formula

    Severance pay = average daily wage × 30 × days of continuous service ÷ 365; average daily wage is total wages over the previous 3 months divided by the total days in that period. If ordinary wages are higher, use the ordinary wage.

  • Ministry of Labor and Employment — 1-year and 15-hour weekly requirement for retirement benefits

    Need for separate judgment for cases where continuous labor of 1 year and average weekly labor time of 15 hours in 4 weeks are combined with periods less than 15 hours.
