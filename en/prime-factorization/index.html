Factor integer into prime factors

Divide by 2 as a divisor and check the square root of the remainder

Input example

360 = 2³ × 3² × 5

360 is 2×2×2×3×3×5. Counting the repetitions of each prime factor gives 2³×3²×5.

How to use

Frequently Asked Questions

What does it mean when only one number appears in the result?

The entered number is a fraction, meaning it cannot be broken down into a smaller integer. This tool targets integers of 2 or more and up to 1 trillion. Processing for large fractions may take longer.
