Frequently asked questions
Are images and table layout preserved?
No. Only PDF text layers are extracted. Columns and table reading order may change; images, scans and attachments are excluded. Blank text pages are skipped, so compare against the source.
Do all AI services count the same tokens?
No. The full exported Markdown is counted with the selected o200k_base or cl100k_base encoding. Other encodings, system instructions, message wrappers and output reserves are excluded. Leave room within your service’s actual limit.
Are files sent to an AI automatically?
No. Processing and ZIP creation happen in your browser. The ZIP includes input filenames and extracted content: review private information before uploading elsewhere. Limits are 10 files, 25MiB, 200 total sections and 200,000 characters, with 100 pages per PDF.
