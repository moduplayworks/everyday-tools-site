Calculation method and criteria

This tool calculates age and the next birthday based on whether the birthday has occurred and the rule you choose for leap-day birthdays.

Actual example

Someone born on 2000-10-04 is 25 years old as of 2026-10-03, with 1 day until their birthday. Someone born on 2000-10-03 is 26 years old on that date; their birthday is today.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the age calculated?

Calculate age as the reference year minus the birth year, subtracting 1 if the birthday has not yet occurred that year. The birthday itself marks the age increase.

When is February 29 birthday counted in a common year?

You can choose 1st day of March or 28th February. The default is 1st day of March, but it is only a calculation assumption, not a function to set legal or institutional standards.

How are the next birthday and elapsed days counted?

If the standard date is a birthday, it is displayed as today with 0 days remaining. From birth date, the birth date is set to 0 days; time, time zone, and qualification are not reflected.
