How the calculation works

Calculate tax, tip, the total, and per-person shares from a pre-tax amount and rates you enter.

Tax = pre-tax subtotal × tax rate · Tip = selected basis × tip rate · Total = subtotal + tax + tip

How to use

  1. Enter the pre-tax amount and currency code from your receipt.
  2. Enter the tax and tip rates, then choose whether to calculate the tip before or after tax.
  3. Set the number of people and calculate to see the tax, tip, total, and per-person amounts that add up exactly.

Input example

With a USD 80.00 subtotal, 8.25% sales tax, an 18% tip, and 3 people, tax is USD 6.60, the pre-tax tip is USD 14.40, and the total is USD 101.00. Two people pay USD 33.67 and one pays USD 33.66, for an exact total of USD 101.00.

Tax and tip rates vary by location, service, and receipt. This tool does not recommend rates or look up taxes.

Frequently asked questions

Should I calculate tip before or after tax?

Choose the basis that matches your receipt or preference. Practices vary by location and service, so this calculator does not recommend one method.

Does the calculator look up currency or sales tax?

No. The currency code controls display and decimal precision only; there is no currency conversion. Enter the pre-tax amount and tax rate from your receipt. Inputs stay in your browser.

What if the per-person shares do not divide evenly?

The total is split into whole currency units, then any remainder is distributed one unit at a time in list order. The calculator does not collect names or assign extra amounts to specific people.

Can I calculate from a tax-inclusive total only?

Enter the amount you know as the subtotal and set the tax rate to 0 to calculate a tip on that amount. If you know the pre-tax subtotal, enter the actual rate from your receipt.
