Instructions and example

Enumerate valid 0/1/− implicants; cover DP minimizes literal count, then term count.

With 3 variables A,B,C, true minterms 1,3,5,7 reduce to C with 1 literal.

Use 1–4 variables with indices 0–2ⁿ−1. The true set must be nonempty and disjoint from don’t-cares. Hardware delay, hazards and multilevel logic are not optimized. Floating-point results display at most 12 significant digits.

Frequently asked questions

What is the bit order of minterm indices?

A is the most significant bit. ! denotes negation, · means AND and + means OR.

Must don’t-care terms evaluate to true?

No. They may be true or false when simplifying; every required true minterm is covered.
